### Aeroecology Program at U of Delaware

Jeffrey Buller

- 1/3 of US covered by radar - continuous data for >30 years
- Since 2008, 250m pixel resolution
  - scan every 5-10 minutes
- During migration, probably more biology than meterology in the data.
- [Stopover Hotspot Patterns and Protection Status](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.22306738)

![Stopovers appear to be near shorelines](image-6.png)

- Some radar networks in Mexico, Brazil, not as openly available.
- Canada working to make their network available.
